### Limitations of Static Estimates
1. **Traffic Variability:** Chennai traffic varies considerably between peak hours (8:30 AM–11:00 AM, 5:30 PM–8:30 PM) and off-peak periods. Estimates represent typical midday/off-peak logistics transit times.
2. **Monsoon / Weather Conditions:** Road conditions during Northeast Monsoon months (October–December) can alter transit durations along key arterial corridors (such as OMR and GST Road).
3. **Deterministic Matching Engine:** The recommendation engine accounts for travel duration by requiring that remaining food freshness exceeds estimated travel time with a safety buffer.
